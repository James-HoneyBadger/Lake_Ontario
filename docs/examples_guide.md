# Lake Ontario BASIC Examples Guide

This guide walks through example programs included in `examples/`.

## Example scripts

### `hello.lo`
A simple greeting script that introduces Lake Ontario BASIC, evidence-based printing, and interpreter execution.

### `honey_badger_mode.lo`
Demonstrates Honey Badger mode, fearless output, and worker solidarity statements.

### `tariffs_and_truth.lo`
Shows fact checking, crowd estimates, and exception-safe `UNIVERSAL_HEALTHCARE` handling.

### `progressive_canada.lo`
Covers Twitter debunking, billionaire taxation, and loop-based provincial policy.

### `climate_policy.lo`
Explains carbon offsets, renewables, and transit fare utilities.

### `mar_a_lago_audit.lo`
Demonstrates document retrieval and `GOLF_VACATION` pause semantics.

### `medicare_expansion.lo`
Defends public healthcare policy and Honey Badger-style policymaking.

### `electoral_fact_check.lo`
Validates election claims with `FACT_CHECK` and truth meter logic.

### `full_language_tour.lo`
A comprehensive tour of the language including control flow, functions, loops, and file I/O.

### `farcical_policy.lo`
A satirical policy example with apologies, redistribution, and protest forecast humor.

### `gui_demo.lo`
A GUI-specific demo illustrating canvas drawing, input boxes, and graphics commands.

### `graphics_and_sound_showcase.lo`
A rally-visualizer demo covering the full graphics suite (points, ovals, arcs,
polygons, triangles, pen width) plus the sound suite (`PLAY_TONE`,
`PLAY_SOUND_FILE`, `STOP_SOUND`, and the themed `HONEY_BADGER_GROWL` /
`TOWN_HALL_BELL` / `STANDING_OVATION` cues).

### `coalition_case.lo`
`COALITION` / `PLANK` / `MINORITY_REPORT` decision making: comparison bands on a
vote share, string equality on a riding name, and a no-match fallthrough.

### `demo_algorithms.lo`
Classic computer science in a civic accent: Fibonacci, a prime sieve,
frequency counting, and a `SUBPOENA` grading subroutine.

### `demo_data_analysis.lo`
An end-to-end ward analysis pipeline: sorting, averages, per-capita metrics,
and a published report document.

### `demo_new_features.lo`
A focused showcase of `FOR_EACH`, `PERHAPS_ALSO`, string utilities,
`BREAK_FROM_CAUCUS` / `NEXT_MOTION`, type casting, and list indexing.

### `civic_caucus.lo`
A short tour of parliamentary absurdity: `CITIZEN_PETITION`, `HEAR_HEAR`,
`POLL_THE_PEOPLE`, `CIVIC_COMPROMISE`, and `PARLIAMENT_GAZETTE`.

### `climate_and_care.lo`
Climate risk scoring, science consensus, treaty obligations, consultation
requirements, and a funded community care plan.

### `committee_chaos.lo`
Builds a `MUTUAL_AID_REGISTRY`, inspects it with `REGISTRY_KEYS` /
`REGISTRY_VALUES` / `REGISTRY_GET`, and scores a policy against it.

### `community_data.lo`
Median, total, evidence scoring, and `CITIZEN_ANY` / `CITIZEN_ALL` over a
list of attendance figures.

### `community_registry.lo`
Groups citizens by region with `GROUP_BY_CITIZENS` and counts entries with
`COUNT_BY_CITIZENS`.

### `demo_policy_engine.lo`
A multi-branch policy scoring engine with `PERHAPS_ALSO` chains, a `WHILE`
loop, `RANDOM_DEMOCRACY`, `UNIVERSAL_HEALTHCARE`, and
`PUBLISH_RESEARCH_FILE`.

### `policy_standard_library.lo`
The policy built-ins in one place: rigged-election meter, voter suppression
alert, corporate fines, safety audits, land return, and renewable targets.

### `string_and_loops.lo`
String trimming, case conversion, casting, and loop control in one script.

### `worker_services.lo`
Wage theft recovery, affordable housing, accessibility audits,
accommodations, service waits, and emissions reductions.

## Learning path

1. Start with `hello.lo` to confirm the interpreter works.
2. Explore `honey_badger_mode.lo` and `progressive_canada.lo` for domain flavor.
3. Use `full_language_tour.lo` to see advanced control flow.
4. Try `gui_demo.lo` and `graphics_and_sound_showcase.lo` in the GUI IDE for graphics and sound commands.
